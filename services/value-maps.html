Services/Product value maps

Product value maps

A clear, reusable value story for each product, built from the research your team already did and checked with real customers.

Scope this with me Compare all services

At a glance

Step 1 of 3
Where it fits
The starting point for every engagement.
Best for
Companies that have never mapped what their products are worth to a buyer.
Your team keeps
A value map and value messaging for up to three products. More products can be added.

Your product team already knows the value. Nobody has mapped it.

Before a product ships, product engineering researches what customers struggle with, what they need done, and what the product will change for them. That insight rarely reaches a sales conversation.

I don't recreate that work. I turn it into a value map that connects each feature to the business levers it pulls and the KPIs it moves. It's general enough to share with any buyer, and it becomes the foundation for everything that follows.

No math yet, on purpose. Value maps stay qualitative. Numbers come in the toolkit, where every range is labeled by its source.

What you get

  • A value map for each product: features, benefit drivers, value levers, and KPIs
  • Overall value messaging for each product, aligned with product marketing
  • Customer outcome notes that seed future case studies
  • A measurability rating for every KPI
  • A walkthrough of the maps with sales and product marketing

Inputs

Built with the people closest to the product.

Product engineering

The features, the research behind them, and the customer problems they were built to solve.

Product marketing

Current positioning, differentiation, and buyer segments, so the value story matches what's already in market. Anything useful from the maps can flow back into their materials.

Sales

What lands with buyers, the objections that come up, and why deals are won or lost.

3 to 5 customers

The business outcomes they've actually seen, in their own words.

Measurability

Not every KPI can be measured the same way.

Every KPI on the map is rated, so you know from the start what can be proven after go live, and how.

Measured directly

The product reports its own usage and results, such as tickets closed or hours of automation run.

Standard templates

Measured against a baseline

A process KPI captured before and after the sale, such as cycle time or error rate.

Standard templates

Hard to isolate

Outcomes like revenue or margin that many factors move at once.

Reported as a contributing factor, never as proof

How it runs.

  1. 1

    Gather

    Review product research and current messaging with engineering and product marketing.

  2. 2

    Listen

    Interview sales and 3 to 5 customers about the outcomes they've seen.

  3. 3

    Map

    Connect features to benefit drivers, value levers, and KPIs, then rate each KPI.

  4. 4

    Share

    Walk sales and product marketing through the maps and messaging.
