Services/Value realization

Value realization

Prove the value after go live, using baselines captured during the sale and scoped to what each product can actually measure.

Scope this with me Compare all services

At a glance

Where it fits
Optional. Comes after the core steps and after go live.
Best for
Customer success teams heading into renewals and expansion.
Your team keeps
Value review and KPI scorecard templates, and a team trained to run them.

Proof starts during the sale.

Value can only be measured after go live if someone captured the starting point first. The benchmark questionnaires in the toolkit do exactly that.

This engagement teaches your customer success team to measure results against those baselines and present them to customer executives. I help set it up, and your team runs it.

Scope depends on the product. Some products report their own results, some can only be measured against a baseline, and some outcomes can't be isolated at all. Every KPI is rated during mapping, so the scope is clear before you commit.

What you get

  • A value review deck template
  • A KPI scorecard for each product that can be measured
  • Training for customer success on running value reviews
  • A method for turning results into renewal and expansion stories

What can be proven

Honest about what can be measured.

Revenue can rise for many reasons. Proof focuses on the KPIs closest to what the product actually does.

Measured directly

The product reports its own usage and results, such as tickets closed or hours of automation run.

Standard templates

Measured against a baseline

A process KPI captured before and after the sale, such as cycle time or error rate.

Standard templates

Hard to isolate

Outcomes like revenue or margin that many factors move at once.

Reported as a contributing factor, never as proof
