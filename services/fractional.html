Senior value expertise, without a full time hire.

Most growing companies can't justify a full time value engineer yet. Their sellers still need someone to review a business case, sharpen a value story, or keep the tools current.

A fractional retainer gives you that expertise on a set schedule, with capped hours and a fixed term, so it stays focused and predictable.

I advise, and your reps own the deals. The retainer supports your team, not individual pursuits.

What you get

  • Set monthly hours on a 3 or 6 month term
  • Regular office hours for sellers
  • Business case reviews before they reach the buyer
  • Coaching on your largest opportunities
  • Updates to value maps, calculators, and AI assistants
  • A hiring plan when you're ready for your own value engineer

How it runs.

  1. 1

    Scope

    Agree on monthly hours, term, and priorities.

  2. 2

    Office hours

    Hold regular sessions sellers can bring questions to.

  3. 3

    Review

    Check business cases and keep the toolkit current.

  4. 4

    Transition

    Help hire and ramp your own value engineer when the time comes.
