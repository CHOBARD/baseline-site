Value engineering toolkit

The tools that put a buyer's own numbers into your value story, with every number traced to a source.

Best for

Your team keeps
ROI calculators, deck templates, one page summaries, and benchmark questionnaires.

Vendor math doesn't survive finance review.

A business case the vendor builds alone reads like a sales document, and finance treats it that way.

The toolkit turns your value maps into tools a seller runs with the buyer, using the buyer's own starting point and conservative ranges, so the result is a number both sides can defend.

The toolkit adds numbers, not new claims. Everything in it traces back to what the value maps already established.

What you get

  • ROI calculators built on your value maps
  • Every range labeled by where it came from
  • Business case deck templates
  • One page value summaries for each product
  • Process maturity and benchmark questionnaires
  • A baseline capture template for measuring value after go live

Credibility

Every number has a source.

Ranges come from the strongest evidence available, in this order. No number is ever made up.

  1. Your data

    Your own records or product usage data.

  2. Your customers' results

    Case studies and outcomes your customers report.

  3. Customer interviews

    What customers tell us directly during mapping.

  4. Market ranges

    Published benchmarks, used only when nothing above exists.

First choiceLast resort

Questionnaires

One questionnaire, three jobs.

Process maturity and benchmark questionnaires are the quiet workhorse of the toolkit. A seller runs them with the buyer early, and they pay off long after the deal closes.

  • They make the business case credible, because it starts from the buyer's own answers
  • They set benchmarks the buyer can compare against
  • They capture the starting point, so value can be measured after go live

How it runs.
