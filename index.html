The problem

Good products still lose when the value story is thin.

Buyers rarely say no to the product. They say no to a number they can't defend to their own leadership.

  • Reps pitch features

    Buyers hear capabilities and do the value math themselves, usually in a way that favors the cheaper option.

  • The business case shows up late

    Built in a rush at the end of the cycle, it reads like vendor math and stalls in finance review.

  • Nobody captures realized value

    Results from happy customers never make it back into the next pitch, so every deal starts from zero.
